Settings

Close protection

Turned on per window from the toolbar icon.
  1. Open the Cordon popup and switch on Protect this window.
  2. A regular, unpinned Cordon tab opens. Click Activate protection once.
  3. You return to your tabs. Chrome now asks before that window closes.

Nothing opens at browser startup. Protection is opt-in and must be turned on again in each browser session.

Privacy and permissions

  • storage keeps settings and snapshots on this device.
  • tabs reads tab URLs and titles to build snapshots and find the protection tab. Chrome describes this as "read your browsing history". Nothing leaves your device: no analytics, accounts or network requests.
  • Only http(s) tabs in normal windows are saved; incognito is excluded. Snapshots hold full URLs, so clear them on shared computers.

Limits

  • Extensions cannot intercept the X button. Protection relies on Chrome's own confirmation, which needs your click and uses Chrome's generic wording.
  • Crashes, force quit, OS shutdown and some keyboard exits may skip the prompt. Incognito windows are not supported.
  • Restoring a large session opens every tab at once and can use a lot of memory.
